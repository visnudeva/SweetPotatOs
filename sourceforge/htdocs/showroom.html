Themes

Mod+Ctrl+t opens SweetPotato themer: the dark and light presets, plus sliders for the accent and highlight. Apply commits the choice. The same controls are below. A slider keeps that preset’s lightness and saturation and changes the hue.

Wallpapers

New installs start on Neon kanji default. On the desktop, Mod+Ctrl+w opens the picker. Rest the pointer on either side to move through them. The one in front opens here, and you can save the original from that view.
