SweetPotatOs is an Arch-based Linux distro: a live graphical ISO with the SweetPotato Swirl desktop and a Calamares installer, built to revive slow old potato PCs and make them sweet again.

Swirl (https://github.com/visnudeva/swirl) is the scrolling tiling Wayland compositor — columns on a horizontal strip, dynamic workspaces, Sway-compatible config, stock swaybar/swaymsg/swaynag.

Default apps include Spore (web radio + local music; Mod+r), LocalSend (nearby file sharing), Firefox, foot, Thunar, Geany, Shelly, and waypaper. Spore and LocalSend replace tera and Audacious from earlier releases.
